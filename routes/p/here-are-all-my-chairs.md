# Here are all my chairs

I put them all in the chair area of my apartment.

Here's where all the tables are. Right across the hall.

Here's my mattress corner and there's my bed.

Everything is neatly organized according to function. I can find everything I need easily.

How do I have lunch? I grab a chair from the chair area and bring it to the dining area.
Then I go to the tables area and grab my dining table, move it across the hall and put it
in the dining area. Have lunch, put everything back in its spot and I'm done. Easy peasy.

I'm the most organised person I know. This is the architecture of my home. Feel free to 
copy this in if you want to be clean and organised like me.
